4 — Gang Count Scenarios Board corrected 9 Jun
🔵 Why previous figures were wrong — Board finding
Original 2-gang = 18 weeks: Wrong. Derived by simply halving Simon's 32-week estimate. Does not account for sequential constraints within each phase or the physical limits of a ring road site.
The ring road has two phases (Phase 1: S36→S25 / Phase 2: S41→S55) that genuinely can run in parallel. That's the efficiency gain from 2 gangs — not halving the total, but running the phases concurrently.
300mm and 450mm pipe (404m total) is significantly slower per metre than smaller pipe — wider trench, deeper, more backfill, needs 21T excavator. Can't simply double this with more gangs. One machine, one trench.
Prelims don't scale. Simon priced Site Manager at 50% time, 1 set of plant. Running 2 gangs = SM at 100% + 2nd excavator + 2nd dumper + 2nd drag box. ~£66k additional cost NOT in the current tender. Must add to prelims before submission if 2-gang working is planned.
Inspection bottleneck. Dandara must sign off manholes, drainage, CCTV before progression. Two gangs = two inspection queues. On IoM, Dandara's site team pace limits concurrent inspection throughput.
Floor constraint. CCTV survey, air/water testing, Dandara sign-off — these take time regardless of gang count. Programme cannot realistically go below ~15-16 weeks even with unlimited gangs.

🔵 Why "same team" doesn't give a proportional time saving
BA4 gang-weeks: 2.5 gangs × 13 weeks = 32.5 gang-weeks of work for a £469k job
GF2 difficulty-adjusted multiplier: scope is 2.8× BA4 by value, but adjusted to ~3.0× for large pipe difficulty (300/450mm), ring road logistics, and inspection flow constraints
GF2 gang-weeks needed: 32.5 × 3.0 = ~98 gang-weeks
With 2.5 gangs (same team): 98 ÷ 2.5 = ~39 weeks. Ring road's parallel phases compress this to ~32 weeks — matching Simon's estimate almost exactly.
Conclusion: Simon's 32-week 1-gang estimate and the BA4-benchmarked 2.5-gang team both land at ~32 weeks. The two methods agree. This gives high confidence in the programme.
