Shareholders agreement — five protections

A shareholders agreement is drafted and signed before any money moves. All five terms are standard and non-onerous for DGC. Estimated cost: £2,000 from an IoM solicitor, 2–3 weeks to complete.

1 · Tag-along rights
If DGC is sold, Daniel sells at the same price per share. He can never be excluded from a sale — his equity rides alongside Ash and Harry’s in any transaction.
2 · Pre-emption on new shares
If DGC raises capital in future by issuing new shares, Daniel has first right to participate and maintain his equity percentage. No silent dilution without notice.
3 · Take out policy documented
Annual review. Written target: 40–50% of profit distributed when cashflow allows. Not a legally binding obligation — but written and signed. Removes ambiguity for all parties.
4 · Buyback formula confirmed
DGC can buy Daniel out at 4× Profit × his equity % at any time from Year 5. The formula is written into the agreement — no negotiation, no dispute, calculated from audited accounts.
5 · Reserved matters
Both Ash and Harry must agree in writing before: taking on debt above £500k, selling the business or a material asset, issuing new shares, or changing the dividend policy. Daniel is notified but has no veto.
Cost & what comes first
IoM solicitor — approx. £2,000 — 2–3 weeks. Accountant valuation note at share issuance (approx. £300). Both required before money moves. SHA protects DGC as much as it protects Daniel.
